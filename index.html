👶 Baby's Journey in Mom's Womb

Click each month to see baby’s development inside mom

Fertilization and implantation. Baby starts developing the neural tube (brain and spinal cord).

Heart starts beating. Basic facial features and limb buds form.

Organs start to develop, fingers and toes begin to form, and baby starts moving.

Baby's bones harden. Facial features become clearer, and baby can suck and swallow.

Baby’s movements are noticeable (kicking). Hair, eyebrows, and eyelashes grow.

Baby’s lungs develop. Skin is translucent but starts to thicken.

Baby responds to sounds. Rapid brain development and muscle tone increases.

Baby gains weight quickly. Most organs are developed and functional.

Baby prepares for birth by positioning head downward. Skin becomes smooth and baby gains fat.

Final development stage. Baby is fully developed and ready to be born.
